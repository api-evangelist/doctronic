# Disclosure Policy
We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours and provide updates every 7 days. Please allow 90 days before public disclosure.

# Scope
In scope: www.doctronic.ai, cms.doctronic.ai, api.doctronic.ai
Out of scope: Social engineering, physical attacks, third-party services

# Safe Harbor
We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:
- Make good faith efforts to comply with this policy
- Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate patient data
- Do not perform testing that degrades services

# Acknowledgments
We privately thank researchers and may provide tokens of appreciation for valid reports. Public acknowledgment available upon request after issue resolution.
